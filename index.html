FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SUM FIBERNET plans and support.

Our technicians typically complete installation within 1–2 hours of arrival. For complex setups like office networks or multi-floor apartments, it may take up to 3–4 hours. We offer same-day and next-day installation slots.
All our plans come with truly unlimited data — no Fair Usage Policy (FUP) caps or speed throttling after reaching a threshold. You get full advertised speed throughout the entire billing month.
Basic and Standard plans include a dual-band WiFi 5 router. Premium plans include a tri-band mesh router system. Business plans receive enterprise-grade routers with management dashboards. All routers are provided free with active subscriptions.
Call or WhatsApp our support line — we aim to resolve 90% of issues remotely within 1 hour. If an on-site visit is needed, our technician will arrive within 4–8 hours. Business plan customers receive priority response under our SLA.
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade your plan at any time and the change takes effect within the same billing cycle with prorated charges. Downgrades take effect from the next billing cycle. No penalties, no exit charges.
Yes. We service apartments, gated societies, individual homes, shops, and offices. For large housing societies, we offer bulk connection packages at discounted rates. Contact us for a custom quote.
Monthly plans have no lock-in period — cancel anytime with 7 days notice. Annual plans come with a 12-month commitment and offer up to 20% savings. We don't charge cancellation fees on monthly plans.
